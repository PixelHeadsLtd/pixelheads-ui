Important information regarding forms

All form elements must be nested inside a div with the class .field, if there is too much margin (usually on the first field in the group), please add the additional class .boxed. This also applies to the first H2 on the page.

Nested inside this div and in the following order, you should have a label, input and any validation message. The validation div is triggered by the class name .error appended to the wrapping field div and an additional *ngIf added to the error message div itself. How to structure your page (scroll down to see ag-grid instructions)

Page header

Both the page-header and left-nav are part of core-framework

Left nav

class=`control-bar`

Your page title goes here (h1)

`aa-tab-navigation` and/or `aa-filter` sits between the control-bar and the content-container

class=`content-container`

Your section title goes here (h2)

To ensure the page has the correct padding, make sure you wrap everything inside a 'content-container'

How to structure your page using ag-grid

Page header

Both the page-header and left-nav are part of core-framework

Left nav

class=`control-bar`

Your page title goes here (h1)

`aa-tab-navigation` and/or `aa-filter` between control-bar & content-container

class='content-container grid ag-grid-full-height' [ngStyle] here

class=`content-container`

Your section title goes here (h2)

Other page content must be wrapped inside another content-container this will ensure that the correct page padding is maintained

class=`ag-grid-wrapper`

Ag-grid

So that ag-grid displays properly, filling the screen horizontally and vertically, you must add the 'grid ag-grid-full-height' class and your ngStyle setting to the top-level content-container, as seen above in orange
