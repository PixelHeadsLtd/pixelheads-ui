Modifying the App logo

We've made the App logo much easier to update. We now use the Material Icons for the specific App branding. Just pass in your chosen icon friendy name.

Updating your App logo colours

You will then need to decide on the background, foreground and border colours for your chosen App branding. The list of available colour classnames can be seen in the Attributes table on the right.
