Ng-select

For selects that use a typeahead or other features such as tags, please use ng-select.

After installing the latest ng-select package in your packages.json, please remember to point to the node_modules ng-select default.theme.css by adding this at the top of your styles.scss file: @import "~@ng-select/ng-select/themes/default.theme.css";
